# Refund Policy

**Last updated:** 9 August 2026  
**Effective date:** 9 August 2026  
**Applies to:** paid Divvylore subscriptions purchased through https://divvylore.com, https://app.divvylore.com, or related Divvylore checkout flows

This Refund Policy is part of our [Terms and Conditions](/terms-and-conditions). If there is a conflict on refunds, this Policy controls for that topic. Capitalised terms not defined here have the meaning in the Terms.

We aim to keep refunds **customer-friendly and flexible**, while preventing abuse and remaining fair for long-term use.

---

## 1. How to request a refund

Email [hello@divvylore.com](mailto:hello@divvylore.com) from the account billing email with:

- Tenant / organisation name  
- Account email  
- Invoice or payment reference (if available)  
- Reason for the request (optional, but helps us improve)

We will normally respond within **seven (7) business days**. Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method via our payment processor and may take additional processor/bank time to appear.

---

## 2. Flexible refunds (first 90 days)

If your Tenant’s **first paid subscription start date** is **ninety (90) days or fewer** before the refund request date (the “**Flexibility Window**”), you may request a refund of **eligible prepaid fees** for the Services, and we will generally approve it in good faith.

During the Flexibility Window we will typically refund:

- The **unused portion** of the current prepaid billing period; and  
- Where you paid annually (or another multi-month prepaid term) and cancel within the Flexibility Window, a **full refund of unused prepaid time** for that term, calculated fairly from the request date.

We may, at our discretion, refund the **full amount of the most recent prepaid charge** during the Flexibility Window when that is simpler and still reasonable.

---

## 3. Prorated refunds (after 90 days)

If your Tenant has been on a **paid subscription for more than ninety (90) days** since the first paid subscription start date, refunds (if approved) are on a **prorated** basis only:

- We refund the **unused remaining portion** of the current prepaid billing period, measured from the refund approval / cancellation effective date through the end of that period.  
- Time already used in the current period is **not** refundable.  
- Prior completed billing periods are **not** refundable.  
- Example: on an annual plan with 4 months remaining after day 91, an approved refund covers those remaining months (less any non-refundable add-ons or usage charges described below), not the full year.

---

## 4. What is not refundable

Unless required by law, the following are not refundable (or are deducted from any refund):

- Fees for periods already fully used  
- One-time setup, onboarding, professional services, custom development, or marketplace/third-party pass-through fees (unless we expressly say otherwise in writing)  
- Overage, usage-based, or metered charges already incurred  
- Taxes remitted to tax authorities that cannot be recovered  
- Payments obtained by fraud, chargeback abuse, or Terms violations  
- Free trials, free plans, promotional credits, and beta features provided at no charge  

---

## 5. Cancellation vs refund

- **Cancel** stops future renewals. You keep access until the end of the paid period unless we refund and terminate earlier.  
- A **refund request** may include a request to cancel and end access when the refund is processed.  
- Cancelling alone does not automatically create a refund; you must request one under this Policy.

---

## 6. Good-faith discretion and abuse

We reserve the right to:

- Ask for reasonable verification of account ownership  
- Deny or reduce refunds where we reasonably believe there is abuse (for example repeated subscribe–refund cycles, chargebacks instead of contacting us, or clear Terms violations)  
- Offset amounts you owe us against any refund  
- Offer account credit instead of a cash refund where you prefer that  

Nothing in this Policy limits rights you cannot waive under mandatory consumer law.

---

## 7. Chargebacks

Please contact us before filing a payment dispute. Filing a chargeback without first giving us a reasonable chance to resolve the issue may result in suspension of the account while the dispute is pending.
